Our Story

Kezmalati Enterprise was founded on a simple belief: every home, office and business deserves security and power solutions they can truly rely on. What started as a small supply operation has grown into a trusted name for premium security doors and soundproof Perkins diesel generators across Lagos and beyond.

Over the years, we've built our reputation one installation at a time — sourcing high-grade materials, working with skilled technicians, and standing behind every product we sell with genuine after-sales support.
